Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2024

NotifIQ is designed with privacy as a core principle. This document explains exactly what data we collect, how we handle it, and what we absolutely do not collect.

1. What Data We Collect

NotifIQ reads the content of your incoming Android notifications, including:

  • App name (e.g., "WhatsApp", "Gmail")
  • Notification title
  • Notification body text
  • Timestamp of when the notification was posted

This data is used to classify notifications into categories (Important, Useful, Normal, Low Value, Spam) and display them in your private inbox within the app.

Local Processing Only: All classification happens on your device. No notification content is sent to any external service for processing.

2. How Data Is Stored

All data is stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database. The encryption key is derived from your device's hardware-backed security features.

No cloud upload. No server sync. No third-party storage.

Your notification history remains entirely on your device until you choose to delete it.

3. What We Do NOT Collect

We are committed to collecting zero personal or sensitive data beyond what's needed for notification classification:

  • We do not collect your location
  • We do not access your contacts
  • We do not access your photos or media
  • We do not track your browsing history
  • We do not collect any personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • We do not use any analytics services
  • We do not include any advertising SDKs
  • We do not use any tracking or telemetry technologies

4. Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • View all data: All stored notifications are visible in the app's Inbox screen
  • Delete all data: You can permanently erase all stored notifications, rules, and preferences from the Settings screen
  • Export your data: You can export your notification history as a JSON file at any time
  • Revoke access: You can revoke NotifIQ's notification access at any time from your device's system settings (Settings > Apps > NotifIQ > Notifications)

5. Data Retention

Notification data is automatically deleted after your configured retention period.

  • Default retention: 30 days
  • You can change this in Settings (Data & Privacy section)
  • Available options: 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, or 1 year

When data is deleted, it is permanently removed from your device and cannot be recovered.

6. Third-Party Sharing

We never share your data with any third party. Period.

NotifIQ does not include any code that communicates with external servers (except for standard Android system updates). There are no:

  • Analytics integrations
  • Crash reporting services
  • Advertising networks
  • Cloud backup services
  • Social media integrations

7. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy in the future:

  • The updated policy will be included with the app update
  • We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this document
  • For significant changes, we will notify users through the app

8. Contact

If you have any questions, concerns, or suggestions regarding this privacy policy or NotifIQ's data practices, please contact us at:

privacy@notifiq.app

NotifIQ is built with the belief that your notification data belongs to you and you alone.
